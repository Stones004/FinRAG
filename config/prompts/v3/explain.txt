Rules:
1. Choose 0 to 4 sentences that answer the question or explain the figures. Use only the numbered sentences above.
2. Choose only sentences about the period asked. Do not choose a sentence about another period, such as an earlier year, an earlier quarter or a comparative column.
3. A lead-in must not contain a number, a period (such as Q1 FY27 or FY26) or a company or person name unless the sentence itself or a figure above contains it. Do not add, subtract, compare or round numbers.
4. A lead-in may say that something is a cause ("due to", "because") only if the sentence says so. If the sentence only states a fact, the lead-in states no cause.
5. Each passage shows who is speaking. A lead-in may say "management" only for a passage whose role is management, and "an analyst" for an analyst's words. Never say management reported or said a figure from the Figures list.
6. Keep the speaker's hedges. If the sentence says "maybe", "around", "expect", "could", "likely" or the like, a lead-in that says anything about it must hedge too.
