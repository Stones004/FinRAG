# M08 — Narrative Indexing and Search

**Package:** `src/finrag/narrative/` · **Depends on:** M02, M03 · **Tests:** `tests/m08_narrative/`

## Purpose
Turn earnings call transcripts, the XBRL text blocks of results filings and the notes/auditor's-report pages of results PDFs into section-aware, cited chunks, and search them with filters + BM25 + embeddings. Every chunk traces back to a filing and a page (or, for an XBRL text block, the concept it came from).

## Source policy (decision 2026-10-13)
v1 indexes three kinds of source:
- **Transcripts** (`earnings_call_transcript`, `source_type = transcript`) are the default narrative source for quarterly "why" questions. All transcript pages are indexed.
- **XBRL text blocks** of results filings (`source_type = results`; no page: `page` is NULL and the citation names the filing and the XBRL concept). Read from `processed/<filing_id>/text_facts.jsonl` (M03). Only these concepts are indexed (names seen in the real filings; a block that is absent in a filing is simply absent):
  - `in-capmkt:DisclosureOfNotesOnFinancialResultsExplanatoryTextBlock` → section `results_notes` (up to ~5,900 characters in the real files);
  - `in-capmkt:DisclosureOfNotesOnSegmentsExplanatoryTextBlock` → `segment_notes`;
  - `in-capmkt:DisclosureOfNotesOnAssetsAndLiabilitiesExplanatoryTextBlock` → `assets_liabilities_notes`;
  - `in-capmkt:DeclarationOfUnmodifiedOpinionOrStatementOnImpactOfAuditQualification` → `audit_opinion` (a short declaration, e.g. "Unmodified opinion"; kept as one chunk although it is under `min_chunk_tokens`).
  Text blocks keep the period, basis and revision of their filing, so a revised filing's blocks supersede the original's.
- **Results PDFs** (quarterly/annual results, `source_type = results`): **only the notes pages and the auditor's / limited-review report pages**, found by the heading patterns `narrative_sections.results_notes` / `auditor_report` (the M03 page classifier only tells statement pages from the rest); the financial statement pages are numbers (M04) and are not indexed as text. Skipped pages are accounted for in the `SkipSummary`.
- **Press releases appended to results PDFs** (decision 2026-10-19; HDFC's "NEWS RELEASE" pages: asset quality, deposits, advances): indexed as section `press_release`, `source_type = results`, basis `unknown`. The release runs from the page whose first lines match `narrative_sections.press_release.start` to the end of the document; statement tables inside it are not indexed (they repeat the results).
- **Annual reports: deferred.** They stay in the raw store (M02 still fetches and registers them) but M08 does not section, chunk or index them (`narrative.index_annual_reports: false`). The design below (sectioning, the `narrative.annual_report_sections_include` whitelist, `SkipSummary`) is kept in this spec marked **deferred, re-enable for the shenanigans layer**; it has no code or tests in v1.
- **Not searchable in v1, by this decision:** contingent liabilities, key audit matters, the CARO annexure, full accounting policies and MD&A / directors' report. A question that needs them gets no narrative evidence and an explicit "not indexed" warning (M09), never a guess. The trade-off is in PROGRESS ("Narrative sources v1").
- `unknown` spans are never indexed; they are skipped and listed so patterns can be improved.

## Inputs
- `processed/<filing_id>/pages.jsonl` from M03. For transcript PDFs M03 also stores per-line span info (`lines: [{text, bold, font, size}]`) so the bold-label rule can run without re-opening the PDF.
- XBRL text blocks from M03: `processed/<filing_id>/text_facts.jsonl` (`{concept, context, period_start, period_end, dimensions, value}`); only entity-total (no dimension) blocks of the concepts listed in the source policy.
- `config/sections.yaml` → `narrative_sections` and `transcript_markers` (added by this module).
- `settings.narrative.index_annual_reports` (false in v1); for the deferred annual-report path `settings.narrative.annual_report_sections_include`; `settings.narrative.log_skipped_sections`.

### `sections.yaml` additions
The real keys are in `config/sections.yaml` (this spec describes them; the file is the source of truth):
- `transcript_markers`: `start`, `footer_line`, `speaker_line`, `not_speaker`, `generic_speakers`, `participant_block`, `participant_entry`, `participant_sep`, `analyst_intro`, `qa_start`, `qa_end`, `operator_phrases`, `furniture_lines`, `furniture_head_lines`, `furniture_min_share`, `lost_label_min_words`. Derived 2026-10-11 from the four real transcripts in `data/inbox/` (HDFC Bank Q4 FY26 / Q1 FY27, Netweb Q4 FY26 / Q1 FY27), re-checked 2026-10-19. Two issuers, both moderator-led: re-check with every new issuer; do not assume it generalises. (`management_roles` of the first draft was dropped: roles come from the cover participant block.)
- `narrative_sections` (results PDFs, used now): `results_notes.heading`, `auditor_report.heading` and `.end` (the pattern key was renamed from `results_auditor` to `auditor_report` at the 2026-10-19 review), `notes_continuation` (`pattern`, `min_lines`), `not_narrative_head` (`head_lines`, `patterns`, `tail_patterns`). The annual-report headings (`mdna`, `directors_report`, `caro`, `notes`, `risk`, `corporate_governance`, `related_party`, `contingent`) stay in the file marked **deferred, re-enable for the shenanigans layer**; nothing reads them in v1.
- `xbrl_text_blocks`: the four indexed concepts and their sections.

Notes from the four files (confirmed by reading them):
- **Cover letter first.** Each PDF starts with the company's 1–2 page letter to the exchanges (HDFC's also carries a link to its website); the transcript proper begins at the title block ("<Company> Q1 FY27 Earnings Conference Call"). Cover pages are excluded from chunks.
- **Speaker lines** are `Name:` followed by text; names may be first-name-only. `Moderator:` is the operator; at Netweb the host (an analyst firm, listed under `MODERATOR:` in the cover block) also speaks as host in prepared remarks and **asks questions as an analyst** in Q4 FY26 ("Seema Nayak from ICICI Securities").
- **The analyst intro is not always said by the Moderator.** At Netweb the IR adviser (listed as management) reads "The next question is from the line of …"; intro text can also appear in an unlabeled turn. Scan every turn in Q&A.
- **Intro variants seen:** `First/The first/Next/The next question is from the line of <Name> from <Firm>`; `<Name>, <Firm>` (comma, no "from"); `<Name> from -- who is a Retail Investor` (no firm); first-name-only (`Pranav from Bernstein`); firm wrapping onto a second line (join whitespace before matching); the same analyst re-introduced later for a follow-up (`Jatin Kalra` twice).
- **Label loss at Netweb (text layer):** the PDF's text often drops the speaker label for continuing paragraphs, and some pages list labels stacked at the top (`Moderator: / Seema Nayak: / Sanjeev Sancheti: / Sanjay Lodha:`) with the paragraphs below unlabeled. HDFC transcripts label every turn. See attribution rules below.
- **Page furniture:** `Page N of M` footers (Netweb) and repeating header text (company name, call date) that can sit on the same line as a label (`Moderator:    HDFC Bank Limited`). Strip repeated header text before matching labels.

Starting patterns only; extend from real transcripts. Regexes are applied to whitespace-normalised turn text and must be tested against all four files plus a synthetic fixture.

## Files

### `narrative/sectioner.py`
- **Input:** results-PDF pages (`PageInput(page, text, statement_type, quality)`; `statement_type` from the M03 page classifier) and the compiled `narrative_sections` patterns.
- **Process (results PDFs, v1)** — calibrated on the 12 results PDFs in the inbox (2026-10-19; per-filing table in PROGRESS "M08 notes"). A *heading* is a whole short line matching `results_notes.heading` or `auditor_report.heading`; a sentence that merely mentions the auditor is not one.
  - A heading starts a span (`results_notes` or `auditor_report`) **from that line on**. On a page that is a financial statement, the text before the heading is dropped (the notes heading very often sits at the bottom of a statement page); such pages are listed as `partial_pages`. (This extends the first decision "headings on unclassified pages only": 6 of the 12 PDFs put the notes heading on a page the classifier labels P&L or cash flow.)
  - A span runs over the following pages that are not statements, until: a statement page without a heading; a page that opens an annexure / covering letter (`not_narrative_head`, among its first `head_lines` lines); the page carrying `UDIN` (auditor report, inclusive); or a **press release** (`press_release.start`, e.g. "NEWS RELEASE"), which runs to the end of the document as section `press_release` (its "Note:" footnotes and numbered lines belong to it, not to the notes of the results).
  - **Numbered notes continue without a heading**, also after a statement page has interrupted them (HDFC: notes, cash-flow page, notes 7-11): an unclassified page with at least `notes_continuation.min_lines` numbered lines continues the notes if a notes heading was seen and no annexure / press release / end of auditor report has come since.
  - Every other page is skipped and recorded: `not_indexed` (statement, annexure, press release) or `unknown_section` (covering letters and anything unclassified outside a span), with the first line of the first page as `detail` so patterns can be improved.
- **Process (annual reports) — DEFERRED, not built in v1:** detect heading lines (line-level: short, matches pattern, often uppercase/title-case); a section runs until the next detected heading; pages before any match → `unknown`. Also use the report's table of contents if detectable. **All sections are detected**, whitelisted or not.
- **Basis of a slice (decision 2026-10-19):** taken from the page heading (the M03 classifier's basis for the page: exactly one of consolidated / standalone in its first lines). A page whose heading names none takes the basis of the latest page that did, in document order (HDFC's PDF is the standalone results, then the consolidated results: continuation pages belong to the block before them), reset by an annexure; a press release is `unknown`; only when no page before it named one does the slice fall back to the filing's basis. A chunk never crosses a basis change. (Deviation from the instruction "fall back to the filing basis only when the page has none": the carry-over is between the page heading and the filing fallback, because the filing basis is wrong for half of HDFC's PDF.)
- **Output:** `(list[PageSlice(page, section, text, quality, partial, basis)], SkipSummary)`; `spans_of()` turns slices into `SectionSpan(section, start_page, end_page)`.
- **Whitelist (DEFERRED with annual reports):** `partition(spans, include) -> (kept_spans, SkipSummary)`; spans whose `section` is not in `narrative.annual_report_sections_include` go to the summary with `reason = not_whitelisted`. Not built in v1.
- **`SkipSummary`:** per filing, list of `{start_page, end_page, reason, detail}` (`reason` ∈ `not_indexed` | `unknown_section`; `not_whitelisted` with the deferred path; an annual-report filing is reported as `deferred`) plus totals (pages kept / skipped / total) and `partial_pages`. Written to `processed/<filing_id>/narrative_summary.json`; with `narrative.log_skipped_sections` false, skipped pages are still excluded but only the totals are kept. Pages must always be accounted for: kept + skipped = total (transcripts too: the cover letter and title page are `not_indexed`).
- **Reading the result:** `finrag narrative sections [--isin X]` prints, per results PDF, the notes pages, the auditor/review report pages, their text quality and the skipped pages; where none is found it says `none found`. Nothing is assumed.

### `narrative/transcript_sectioner.py`
- **Input:** transcript pages, `transcript_markers`.
- **Process:**
  1. Drop cover-letter pages (before `start`) and `footer_line`s; strip repeated header text. Cover-letter text is kept for the link-only check (M02).
  2. Read the cover participant block (`participant_block`, `participant_sep`) → name → role/company map (`MANAGEMENT` → management, `MODERATOR` → moderator).
  3. Segment into turns by `speaker_line` (excluding `not_speaker`). The stacked-label case of the first draft (several `Name:` lines followed by unlabeled paragraphs) did not occur in the four files and is not implemented; text without a label stays with the preceding turn, so the only unlabeled-turn checks are rule 6 below and the bold test of 3b.
  3b. **Font information (verified 2026-10-19 on the four files).** On a native page every real speaker label is a bold line start (`lines[].bold`, stored by M03), in HDFC and in Netweb. Rules: a row is a label if the text pattern matches and the row is bold; a **bold row ending in `:`** is a label even if the pattern missed it (e.g. a lower-case name); a **label-shaped row that is not bold** is accepted only when its name is on the cover participant list (listed in the summary as `non_bold_labels`; Netweb Q1 FY27 p.14 "Hirdey Vikram:"), otherwise it starts an **unattributed turn** (`speaker = NULL`, reported). Pages without font info (OCR) use the text pattern alone. The first draft's "labels lost in the text layer" (PROGRESS finding 15) was a property of the plain-text extraction: with the line layer no label was lost in the four files.
  4. Split sections: `prepared_remarks` = from `start` to the turn containing the first `qa_start` match; `qa` = from that turn up to the closing hand-over (`qa_end`); `closing_remarks` = everything after the hand-over (management's closing words, which often contain guidance such as "we expect…"). The operator's sign-off ("that concludes this conference") is excluded as an operator turn. No `qa_start` match → everything is `prepared_remarks` with a warning in the summary. No `qa_end` match → no `closing_remarks` section (nothing is guessed), and a warning is recorded.
  5. **Roles** (`speaker_role` ∈ management | analyst | moderator), first rule that applies:
     a. label `Moderator` → moderator; label `Management` → management (speaker kept as "Management");
     b. a name found by an `analyst_intro` match → **analyst from that intro onward** (matched by full name, or by first name when the intro gave only a first name), with the firm stored. This overrides the participant list (the Netweb host is a moderator until she is introduced as an analyst);
     c. a name in the participant block → its listed role;
     d. any other speaker before the first analyst intro → management; any other speaker after it who is not an analyst → management only if they answer an analyst turn, else NULL;
     e. otherwise `speaker_role = NULL`, listed in the summary. Never guess.
     Deviation from the first draft: after the first analyst intro a speaker who is not an analyst is management when the previous non-operator turn was an analyst **or management** (several answerers in a row), not only an analyst.
  6. **Lost analyst label.** When an operator or IR-adviser turn holds an intro and, after the intro sentence and operator phrases (`operator_phrases`: "please go ahead", "unmute", ...), at least `lost_label_min_words` words, those words are that analyst's question and become a turn of their own; each case is listed as `label_lost_after_intro` in the summary. (Synthetic fixture only; none occurs in the four real files.)
  7. **QA unit** = from an analyst intro through all that analyst's turns, any management replies, and any "we'll wait for your second question"-style turns, ending at the next analyst intro or `qa_end`. A re-introduced analyst starts a new unit. Operator turns (label `Moderator`) and intro sentences are excluded from unit text but the firm is kept. A host who speaks in the prepared remarks (listed under `MODERATOR:`) is kept as a `moderator`-role unit.
- **Output:** `list[TranscriptUnit(section, speaker, speaker_role, firm, start_page, end_page, text, answers)]`. For a QA unit, `speaker`/`speaker_role`/`firm` are the questioner's, `text` is the question (the analyst's turns before the first answer) and `answers` the blocks that follow, in order: `Answer (<name>, <role title>):` per other speaker (`Answer (unattributed):` for an unattributed turn), `Follow-up (<analyst>):` for the analyst's later turns. Prepared-remarks units are one per management turn (the host listed under `MODERATOR:` is kept as a moderator-role unit; the operator, label `Moderator`, is dropped everywhere). Closing remarks are one unit per management turn with `section = closing_remarks`; they are never attached to a question. An intro read by an IR adviser leaves the adviser's earlier words in the previous analyst's unit.
- **Summary:** `narrative_summary.json` lists the cover pages, participants, analysts (name, firm, turn), `unattributed_turns`, `null_role_turns`, `non_bold_labels`, `label_lost_after_intro` and warnings (no Q&A start, no closing hand-over, no title block).

### `narrative/chunker.py`
- **Input:** pages + kept spans (results PDFs), XBRL text blocks, or `TranscriptUnit`s (transcripts).
- **Process:** split text within each section into chunks of `narrative.chunk_tokens` with `chunk_overlap_tokens`, breaking on sentence boundaries; never cross a section boundary; a **tail under `min_chunk_tokens` is merged into the previous chunk, never dropped**; page headers/footers are the lines repeated at the edges of at least 40 % (and 3) of the pages and are stripped first. Token counts are an approximation (words plus punctuation marks), the same everywhere, independent of the embedding model; the model truncates at 512 word-pieces, so a 400-token chunk fits. Each chunk keeps the page where it starts (and end page if it spans). **Transcript Q&A units are cut by turn (changed 2026-10-20; before, a question and its answers shared one chunk labelled with the analyst's role, so management's words carried `speaker_role = analyst`).** The analyst's question is a chunk (`Question (<name>, <firm>): ...`, role `analyst`); each reply is its own chunk (`Answer (<name>, <title>): ...`, speaker = the name in the label, role `management`, `meta.role_title`); the analyst's later turns (`Follow-up (<name>): ...`) are `analyst` chunks; an unattributed reply has no speaker and no role. A long reply is split on sentences and each piece keeps its speaker label. A reply's `meta.question` holds the question (first `chunk_tokens` characters); the **index text** (BM25 and embeddings) of a reply is that question followed by the reply, while the stored and returned chunk text is the reply alone, so what is quoted is what was said. Only indexed spans reach the chunker. An XBRL text block is chunked on paragraph boundaries within itself; a block or declaration shorter than `min_chunk_tokens` is kept whole.
- **Output:** `chunks` rows (schema v6): `chunk_id`, filing_id, isin, fiscal_year, section, `source_type` (`transcript` | `results`; `annual_report` is reserved for the deferred path), **`period_start`, `period_end`, `period_type` (copied from the filing at index time, so a period filter needs no join)**, `speaker`/`speaker_role` (transcript chunks only, else NULL), page (NULL for XBRL text blocks), seq, text, token_count, **`meta_json`** (`basis` - for PDF chunks the page-heading basis above, `unknown` for a press release; the filing's for XBRL text blocks and transcripts; transcripts: the analyst's `firm`, `unit`, `end_page`; PDF chunks: `end_page`; text blocks: the XBRL `concept`, so the citation can name it). Chunk IDs: `CHK-<filing_id>-p<page>-<seq>`, and `CHK-<filing_id>-x-<seq>` for an XBRL text block; `seq` runs over the filing. A chunk of a long Q&A unit carries the unit's first page and `end_page`.

### `narrative/embedder.py`
- **Process:** load `narrative.embedding_model` locally (sentence-transformers, on `embedding_device`, CPU by default); embed in batches of `embedding_batch_size`; normalise vectors. Queries get `narrative.embedding_query_prefix` (BGE's retrieval instruction; blank for models without one). The first use downloads the model once (approved 2026-10-19); tests run offline from the cache.
- **Output:** `indexes/<isin>/vectors.npy` + `ids.json` (model name, chunk IDs, a fingerprint of each chunk's text). The indexes are rebuilt from the `chunks` table; vectors whose chunk ID, text and model are unchanged are reused, a different `embedding_model` re-embeds everything (`narrative index --rebuild-vectors`). Interface `Embedder` (`name`, `embed`, `embed_query`) so it can be mocked.

### `narrative/bm25_index.py`
- **Process:** tokenise (lowercase; numbers like `7,737.02` and hyphenated terms stay whole, a hyphenated word also adds its parts); `rank_bm25` per ISIN; the tokenised corpus is persisted as `indexes/<isin>/bm25.json` (not a pickle: nothing is unpickled).

### `narrative/vector_index.py`
- **Process:** `numpy` cosine search (FAISS was dropped at the review: `vector_backend` accepts `numpy` only); only the chunk IDs that passed the SQL filter are ranked.

### `narrative/search.py`
- **Input:** `SearchRequest(isin, query_text, sections=None, periods=None, fiscal_years=None, source_types=None, k=final_k)`. `periods` is a list of `core.types.Period`: the **exact** period of the source filing (start, end, type), stored on every chunk, so Q4 FY26 and Q1 FY27 chunks of one company never mix; an empty list matches nothing; `None` means no period filter. (`filing_types` of the first draft was dropped: `source_types` says the same.)
- **Process:** (1) SQL filter on `chunks` → candidate IDs (the same filters are re-checked in Python on every hit); (2) BM25 top `top_k_bm25` within candidates (score > 0); (3) dense top `top_k_dense` within candidates; (4) reciprocal rank fusion with `rrf_k`; (5) `final_k`. **Reranking is deferred to M09** (maximal marginal relevance and an optional cross-encoder, see its spec); `narrative.retrieval.reranker_enabled: true` is a configuration error in M08. Chunks stored but missing from the indexes raise `IndexOutOfDate` ("run `finrag narrative index`"), never a silent gap.
- **Output:** `list[SearchHit(chunk_id, filing_id, section, source_type, speaker, speaker_role, firm, page, end_page, concept, basis, fiscal_year, period_start, period_end, period_type, period_label, text, scores)]`. `period_label` (e.g. `Q1 FY27`) comes from the chunk's own period columns, so callers can tag each citation with the quarter it came from; an XBRL text-block hit has `page = None` and names its `concept`. `basis` is returned but not filterable (M09 can drop hits of the other basis).
- **Rule:** never return a chunk outside the filters, even if the result list is empty.

### `narrative/runner.py`
- **Process:** for `parsed`/`normalized` filings (never `quarantined`) that have no chunks: section (transcript sectioner for `earnings_call_transcript`; text-block reader for XBRL results filings; page sectioner for results PDFs; **`annual_report` filings are reported `deferred` while `index_annual_reports` is false**, and `true` is a configuration error because that path is not built) → chunk → replace the filing's `chunks` (deterministic IDs, so re-indexing duplicates nothing) → write the skip summary → rebuild the BM25 and vector indexes of every ISIN whose chunks changed. A filing with a later version (revision) of the same ISIN, type, format, basis and period end is `superseded`: its chunks are removed. One `jobs` row per filing.
- **Commands:** `finrag narrative index [--isin X] [--filing F] [--reindex] [--rebuild-vectors]`, `finrag narrative search --isin X "query" [--section results_notes] [--period "Q1 FY27"] [--fy FY25] [--source transcript] [-k N]`, `finrag narrative skipped --filing FIL-…` (prints the skip summary; `deferred` for annual reports), `finrag narrative sections [--isin X]` (read-only report per results PDF).

## Tests and acceptance criteria
1. Synthetic results-PDF fixture (notes pages, auditor's report pages, statement pages): the notes and auditor's-report spans have the right page ranges; statement pages are skipped and listed.
2. No chunk crosses a section boundary; every PDF/transcript chunk has a page; every XBRL text-block chunk has a NULL page and its concept in the metadata.
3. Repeated page headers/footers are stripped.
4. Filter test: a query matching text in company A never returns company B's chunks; FY filter respected; **the exact-period filter keeps a company's Q4 FY26 and Q1 FY27 chunks apart** (same call text in both).
5. Keyword test: an exact term (e.g. a product name) is found via BM25 even with a mocked embedder returning random vectors.
6. Paraphrase test (optional, marked slow): uses the real model on a fixture.
7. Re-indexing the same filing creates no duplicate chunks.
8. Retrieval eval: `tests/m08_narrative/retrieval_cases.yaml` (question → expected chunk IDs on fixtures); report recall@k.
9. **XBRL text blocks:** from a synthetic `text_facts.jsonl`, exactly the four listed concepts become chunks (sections `results_notes`, `segment_notes`, `assets_liabilities_notes`, `audit_opinion`); metadata facts (dates, names, flags), dimensioned facts and related-party tables are ignored; the one-line audit declaration is kept despite its size; a revision's blocks supersede the original's for the same period and basis.
10. **Skip summary:** for a results PDF the per-filing summary lists each skipped span with section, page range and `reason` (`not_indexed` | `unknown_section`); kept + skipped pages equal the total page count (nothing silently dropped). With `log_skipped_sections: false` the skipped pages are still not indexed and totals are still recorded.
11. **Annual reports are not indexed:** with `index_annual_reports: false`, an `annual_report` filing produces no chunks and no error, stays in the raw store, and `narrative skipped` reports it as `deferred`. (The whitelist tests of the deferred design are written when it is re-enabled.)
12. **Transcript sections:** on a synthetic transcript fixture, everything before the first `qa_start` match is `prepared_remarks`, the Q&A is `qa`, and text after the closing hand-over is `closing_remarks` (never inside `qa`; indexed and searchable by `sections=["closing_remarks"]`); cover-letter pages and `Page N of M` footers are excluded.
13. **Q&A by turn:** every chunk of a Q&A unit has one speaker and a true role (question: analyst; reply: management; follow-up: analyst); a reply chunk never contains the question text but is indexed with it (`meta.question`); a long reply splits with its label kept.
14. **Speaker metadata:** transcript chunks carry `speaker` and `speaker_role` (`management` / `analyst` / `moderator`); roles follow the attribution rules, an unresolvable role is stored as NULL and listed in the summary; non-transcript chunks have both NULL.
15. `source_type` is set on every chunk, and `SearchRequest.source_types=["transcript"]` returns only transcript chunks.
16. **Real-file checks (golden, `@pytest.mark.golden`, skipped if absent):** on the four inbox transcripts, Q&A starts at the first analyst intro; analyst names/firms extracted from the intros match a hand-checked list (including `Pranav`, `Chi Ho Wong`, `Vatsal Aggarwal` with no firm, and a firm wrapped over two lines); `Moderator` turns get role moderator; the Netweb host (`Seema Nayak`) is moderator in prepared remarks and analyst in the Q4 FY26 Q&A; turns whose label was lost are reported in the summary rather than guessed.
17a. Font information (synthetic page records with bold flags): a bold line ending in `:` that the text pattern misses is a label; a label-shaped line that is not bold is reported as unattributed (or accepted and listed when the name is on the cover list), never guessed. The real-file check is in 16 (golden).
17. Analyst intro read by a non-moderator (IR adviser) still starts a QA unit; an analyst re-introduced later starts a new unit.
18. Fixtures for 1, 9–15, 17 are generated by `tests/fixtures/make_narrative.py` (page records and `text_facts.jsonl` of a fictional company; no real transcript or report committed); the synthetic transcript layout is a test fixture only and not evidence of any real transcript format.
19. **Real-file checks (golden):** Netweb's Note 5 (inventory valuation change) is returned for "change in inventory valuation method" from the results PDF (page 9); "asset quality" for HDFC returns HDFC transcript chunks only, of the quarter asked; for every company and period, no hit leaves its company or period filter; all 12 results PDFs yield both a notes span and an auditor/review-report span with page text quality >= 0.70.

## Out of scope
Answer generation (M09).
